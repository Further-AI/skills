# OIG exclusion types

Every code that appears in the `exclusion_type` column, from the Social Security Act sections OIG cites (oig.hhs.gov/exclusions/authorities.asp).

## Mandatory exclusions
| Code | Reason | Minimum period |
|---|---|---|
| 1128a1 | Conviction of program-related crimes | 5 years |
| 1128a2 | Conviction relating to patient abuse or neglect | 5 years |
| 1128a3 | Felony conviction relating to health care fraud | 5 years |
| 1128a4 | Felony conviction relating to a controlled substance | 5 years |

## Permissive exclusions
| Code | Reason | Minimum period |
|---|---|---|
| 1128b1 | Misdemeanor conviction relating to health care fraud (1128(b)(1)(A)), or conviction relating to fraud in a non-health-care program (1128(b)(1)(B)) | 3 years (baseline) |
| 1128b2 | Conviction relating to obstruction of an investigation or audit | 3 years (baseline) |
| 1128b3 | Misdemeanor conviction relating to a controlled substance | 3 years (baseline) |
| 1128b4 | License revocation, suspension or surrender | The period the state licensing authority imposed |
| 1128b5 | Exclusion or suspension under a federal or state health care program | No less than that program's period |
| 1128b6 | Excessive charges, unnecessary services, or care below professional standards; or an HMO failing to furnish medically necessary services | 1 year |
| 1128b7 | Fraud, kickbacks and other prohibited activities | None |
| 1128b8 | Entity controlled by a sanctioned individual | Same as the individual's exclusion |
| 1128b11 | Failure to supply payment information (with 1128(b)(9)-(10): failure to disclose required information or information on subcontractors and suppliers) | None |
| 1128b12 | Failure to grant immediate access | None |
| 1128b14 | Default on a health education loan or scholarship obligation | Until the default is resolved |
| 1128b15 | Individual controlling a sanctioned entity | Same as the entity's exclusion |
| 1128b16 | False statement or misrepresentation of material fact | None |
| 1128Aa | Civil monetary penalty under section 1128A | Set case by case |
| 1156 | Practitioner failed to provide medically necessary care meeting professional standards (Quality Improvement Organization findings) | 1 year |

## Other codes on the list
| Code | Reason |
|---|---|
| BRCH CIA | Breach of a Corporate Integrity Agreement with OIG |
| BRCH SA | Breach of a settlement agreement with OIG |
| 1160 | Not described on OIG's authorities page. Report it as listed and recommend checking the live listing. |

For repeat mandatory offenses, OIG's authorities page sets longer periods: at least 10 years for a second offense and permanent exclusion for a third.

## Reading the codes for underwriting
- **Most serious:**
  - patient harm (1128a2);
  - fraud (1128a1, 1128a3, 1128b1, 1128b7);
  - controlled substances (1128a4, 1128b3).
- **Ownership and control** (1128b8, 1128b15): look for the linked individual or entity too. The person behind an excluded business is often the real exposure.
- **License actions** (1128b4) are the most common listing. Find out why the license was lost, because the reason ranges from administrative to patient harm.
- **Administrative** (1128b14, student-loan default): rarely an underwriting concern on its own.
- **Unknown codes:** a code not in this file may be one OIG added after this list was compiled. Report it as listed and say its meaning wasn't found here.
